## 2. Incorporation Particulars

### 2.1 Objects of the Company

The objects for which the Company is established are:

1. **Primary Object:** To develop, operate, and maintain a video streaming platform ("NovaFlix") enabling creators to publish, monetize, and distribute video content globally.

2. **Secondary Objects:**
   - To provide creator economy tools including subscription management, pay-per-minute viewing, tipping/gifting, membership tiers, merchandise sales, course hosting, and live event ticketing.
   - To operate a multi-gateway payment infrastructure supporting Stripe (global), Paystack (Nigeria), and Flutterwave (Africa) for seamless cross-border transactions.
   - To build and maintain content discovery, recommendation, and social engagement features for viewers.
   - To acquire, license, and distribute film, television, and short-form video content.
   - To provide white-label streaming technology to third parties.
   - To engage in any lawful activity incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects.

### 2.2 Liability of Members

The liability of the members is limited to the amount unpaid on the shares held by them.
