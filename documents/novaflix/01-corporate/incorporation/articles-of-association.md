## 2. Share Capital & Shares

### 2.1 Authorized Capital

₦10,000,000 divided into 10,000,000 Ordinary Shares of ₦1.00 each.

### 2.2 Rights Attaching to Ordinary Shares

Each Ordinary Share confers:
- **Voting:** One vote per share at general meetings
- **Dividends:** Right to participate pro-rata in dividends declared
- **Capital:** Right to participate pro-rata in return of capital on winding up
- **Information:** Right to receive notices, accounts, and reports

### 2.3 Issue of Shares

**2.2.1** The Board may allot shares with prior authorization by ordinary resolution (or per CAMA 2020 Section 124 for private companies).

**2.2.2** Pre-emption rights apply: new shares must first be offered to existing members pro-rata to their holdings (30 days to accept).

**2.2.3** **Parent Company Consent Required:** Any issuance resulting in WID Ltd holding <51% requires WID Ltd written consent.

### 2.4 Transfer of Shares

**2.4.1** Shares are freely transferable subject to:
- Right of first refusal (ROFR) in favor of existing members (30 days)
- Board approval (not to be unreasonably withheld)
- Compliance with securities laws

**2.4.2** Transfers to competitors (as defined by Board) require 75% member approval.

**2.4.3** WID Ltd shares: Any transfer by WID Ltd requires Board resolution but not ROFR.

### 2.5 Share Certificates

Certificates shall be issued within 60 days of allotment/transfer. Replacement for lost certificates per Board procedure.

### 2.6 Lien & Calls

Company has first lien on shares for unpaid calls. Calls may be made by Board with 14 days' notice.

## 3. General Meetings

### 3.1 Annual General Meeting (AGM)

- First AGM within 18 months of incorporation
- Subsequent AGMs within 15 months of prior AGM (not later than 6 months after financial year-end)
- Notice: 21 clear days (or shorter with 95% member consent)

### 3.2 Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM)

- Convened by Board or requisitioned by members holding ≥10% of voting rights
- Notice: 21 clear days (14 days if all members agree)

### 3.3 Notice of Meetings

Notice to: all members, directors, auditors, company secretary. Contents: date, time, venue (physical/virtual), agenda, resolutions, proxy rights.

### 3.4 Quorum

**2 members present in person or by proxy** holding ≥25% of voting shares. If quorum not met within 30 minutes, meeting adjourned 7 days.

### 3.5 Chair

Chairman of Board presides; if absent, directors elect chair; if no director willing, members elect chair.

### 3.6 Voting

- Show of hands (1 vote per person) unless poll demanded
- Poll: 1 vote per share
- Chairman has casting vote on equality
- Proxy votes counted on poll
- Electronic voting permitted

### 3.7 Resolutions

| Type | Threshold | Use Cases |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Ordinary | >50% | Routine matters, director appointments, dividends, auditors |
| Special | ≥75% | Articles amendment, capital changes, winding up, M&A |
| Written | As above | In lieu of meeting (all members sign) |

## 4. Directors

### 4.1 Number of Directors

**Minimum: 2** | **Maximum: 9** (subject to ordinary resolution)

### 4.2 Appointment

| Method | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **First Directors** | Named in incorporation documents |
| **Subsequent** | Ordinary resolution at AGM |
| **WID Nominee** | WID Ltd may appoint 1 director so long as it holds ≥25% |
| **Casual Vacancy** | Board appoints until next AGM |

### 4.3 Qualification

No share qualification required. Directors must be natural persons ≥18 years, not disqualified under CAMA.

### 4.4 Rotation & Retirement

At each AGM, **1/3 of directors** (or nearest number) retire by rotation. Retiring directors eligible for re-election. WID Nominee not subject to rotation.

### 4.5 Removal

- Ordinary resolution (special notice 28 days)
- WID Nominee removable only by WID Ltd written notice

### 4.6 Remuneration

- Executive directors: Per service agreements
- Non-executive: Fees set by ordinary resolution
- Expenses reimbursed
- No loans to directors (except per CAMA exceptions)

### 4.7 Conflicts of Interest

Directors must declare interests (direct/indirect) in transactions. Interested director may not vote, counts toward quorum. Register of interests maintained.

---

## 5. Board Proceedings

### 5.1 Meetings

- **Frequency:** Quarterly minimum; ad hoc as needed
- **Notice:** 7 days (48 hours for emergency); waivable by all directors
- **Quorum:** 2 directors (including ≥1 WID Nominee if appointed)
- **Virtual:** Permitted (video/audio conference)
- **Chair:** Chairman or elected per meeting

### 5.2 Voting

- Majority of directors present
- Chairman has casting vote
- Written resolutions valid if signed by all eligible directors

### 5.3 Committees

Board may establish committees:
- **Audit Committee** (mandatory per CAMA if public — voluntary here)
- **Risk Committee**
- **Compensation Committee**
- **Technology Committee**

Each committee: ≥2 directors, terms of reference by Board.

### 5.4 Delegation

Board may delegate powers to:
- Managing Director / CEO
- Committees
- Officers
- Attorneys (power of attorney)

**Reserved Matters (Board only):**
- Strategy & budget approval
- Capital allocation >₦50M
- Senior appointments (C-suite)
- M&A, JV, partnerships >₦100M
- Articles/Memorandum changes
- Dividend declaration
- Share issuance/buyback
- Related party transactions >₦10M

## 6. Officers

### 6.1 Managing Director / CEO

- Appointed by Board
- Responsible for day-to-day management
- Authority per delegation schedule
- Term: Per service agreement

### 6.2 Company Secretary

- Appointed by Board within 6 months of incorporation
- Duties per CAMA Section 330
- May be individual or firm

### 6.3 Other Officers

CFO, CTO, COO, etc. appointed by Board on CEO recommendation.

---

## 7. Dividends & Reserves

### 7.1 Declaration

Dividends declared by **ordinary resolution** on Board recommendation. Cannot exceed Board recommendation.

### 7.2 Payment

- From profits available for distribution
- Paid pro-rata to shareholdings
- Electronic transfer to registered bank accounts
- Unclaimed dividends: 12 years → forfeited to Company

### 7.3 Reserves

Board may set aside profits as reserves before dividend declaration.

### 7.4 Interim Dividends

Board may pay interim dividends if justified by financial position.

---

## 8. Accounts & Audit

### 8.1 Accounting Records

Kept at registered office (or approved location) for ≥6 years. Comply with IFRS/GAAP.

### 8.2 Financial Statements

Annual: Statement of financial position, profit/loss, cash flows, changes in equity, notes.

### 8.3 Audit

- **Auditor appointed** at AGM (first by Board)
- **Remuneration** fixed by Board
- **Rights:** Access to books, attend meetings, receive notices
- **Exemption:** If eligible as "small company" per CAMA, members may opt out by unanimous consent

### 8.4 Filing

Annual returns + financial statements filed with CAC within 42 days of AGM.

## 9. Capital Structure Protections (Parent Company)

### 9.1 WID Ltd Protective Provisions

So long as WID Ltd holds ≥25% of issued share capital, the following require **WID Ltd prior written consent**:

| Matter | Threshold |
|--------|-----------|
| Amendment of Memorandum/Articles | Any |
| Share issuance / option grants | Any dilution below 51% |
| Share buyback / redemption | Any |
| Dividend policy change | Any |
| Appointment/removal of CEO | Any |
| Budget approval (annual) | Any |
| Capital expenditure | >₦100M single item |
| Borrowing / guarantees | >₦50M aggregate |
| M&A, asset sale, JV | >₦100M |
| Related party transactions | >₦10M |
| Change of business | Any material change |
| Winding up / dissolution | Any |
| Auditor change | Any |
| Registered office change | Any |

### 9.2 Information Rights

WID Ltd entitled to:
- Monthly management accounts (by 15th of following month)
- Quarterly board packs (5 days before meeting)
- Annual audited accounts
- Immediate notice of material events
- Observer at Board meetings (non-voting)

## 10. Common Seal

### 10.1 Seal Custody

Seal kept at registered office in secure custody of Company Secretary.

### 10.2 Affixation

Seal affixed only by Board resolution, witnessed by:
- **Two directors**, OR
- **One director + Company Secretary**

### 10.3 Seal Register

Every affixation recorded in Seal Register with date, document, authorized signatories.

---

## 11. Notices

### 11.1 Methods

- Email (to registered address) — deemed received at sending
- Post (prepaid) — deemed received 48 hours after posting
- Courier — deemed received on delivery
- Electronic (portal/app) — deemed received on notification

### 11.2 Address Updates

Members must update contact details within 14 days of change.

---

## 12. Winding Up

### 12.1 Voluntary

Special resolution → liquidator appointed → assets distributed per CAMA.

### 12.2 Distribution Priority

1. Liquidation costs
2. Creditors (secured → preferential → unsecured)
3. Members: Return of paid-up capital
4. Members: Surplus pro-rata to shareholdings

### 12.3 WID Ltd Priority

WID Ltd shares rank pari passu with other Ordinary shares.

---

## 13. Indemnity & Insurance

### 13.1 Indemnity

Company indemnifies directors/officers against liabilities incurred in good faith execution of duties (to extent permitted by CAMA).

### 13.2 D&O Insurance

Company shall maintain Directors & Officers liability insurance (minimum ₦500M coverage).

---

## 14. Confidentiality

All directors, officers, employees, and agents bound by confidentiality obligations per NDA and Employee Handbook. Trade secrets, algorithms, user data, financials, and strategy protected.

---

## 15. Dispute Resolution

### 15.1 Internal

Disputes between members/directors: Mediation (Lagos Court of Arbitration) → Arbitration (Lagos, English law, 3 arbitrators).

### 15.2 External

Commercial disputes: Lagos State High Court (Commercial Division) exclusive jurisdiction.

## 16. Amendment of Articles

**Special Resolution (75%)** + **WID Ltd written consent** (while WID Ltd holds ≥25%) + CAC filing within 15 days.
