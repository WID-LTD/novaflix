## 6. Future Investment & Dilution

### 6.1 Pre-Emption Rights

On any new share issuance (equity, options, convertibles):
1. Offer to existing shareholders pro-rata (30 days)
2. WID Ltd has **right to oversubscribe** to maintain ≥51%
3. Unsubscribed portion → Board may offer to third parties

### 6.2 Anti-Dilution Protection

**WID Ltd:** Full ratchet anti-dilution until Series A.
**Future Investors:** Broad-based weighted average (standard NVCA).

### 6.3 Employee Share Option Pool (ESOP)

- **Size:** 15% of fully diluted post-Series A
- **Source:** New shares (not from WID Ltd)
- **Administration:** Board Compensation Committee
- **WID Ltd Consent:** Required for pool increases >15%

## 7. Transfer Restrictions

### 7.1 General Restriction

No transfer of shares without:
1. Compliance with Articles (ROFR, Board approval)
2. Transferee executes Deed of Adherence
3. Compliance with securities laws

### 7.2 Permitted Transfers (No ROFR)

| Transferor | Transferee | Conditions |
|------------|------------|------------|
| WID Ltd | WID Ltd affiliate | Same group, same control |
| Key Person | Family trust / estate | For estate planning |
| Any shareholder | Existing shareholder | Pro-rata, same terms |

### 7.3 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)

**Trigger:** Bona fide third-party offer
**Process:**
1. Seller delivers offer notice to Company & shareholders
2. Company has 15 days to purchase (or designate purchaser)
3. Shareholders have 15 days to purchase pro-rata
4. If not fully subscribed → Seller may sell to third party on same terms

### 7.4 Tag-Along Right

If WID Ltd sells >50% of its shares to a third party:
- Minority shareholders may participate pro-rata on same terms
- Trigger: Sale resulting in change of control

### 7.5 Drag-Along Right

If WID Ltd accepts bona fide offer for ≥75% of Company:
- May require all shareholders to sell on same terms
- Notice: 30 days
- Same price, terms, conditions
- Good faith cooperation required
