# Board Charter

> **Governance framework for the Board of Directors of NovaFlix**

---

## 1. Purpose & Authority

### 1.1 Purpose

This Charter establishes the composition, roles, responsibilities, and operating procedures of the Board of Directors of **NovaFlix** ("the Company"), a subsidiary of **WID Ltd (RC 8824091)**.

### 1.2 Legal Authority

Derived from:
- Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA)
- Company's Memorandum & Articles of Association
- Shareholder Agreement (WID Ltd / NovaFlix)
- Nigerian Code of Corporate Governance 2018 (voluntary adoption)

### 1.3 Accountability

The Board is accountable to **WID Ltd** as sole shareholder and acts in the best interests of the Company, considering stakeholders (creators, users, employees, regulators).

| 5 | Investor Director (Series B) | Series B Lead | *Future* | Per investment agreement |

**Maximum:** 7 directors (per Articles)
**Minimum:** 2 directors (per CAMA)

### 2.2 Independence Criteria

A director is "independent" if they:
- Hold no executive position in Company or WID Ltd
- Not a substantial shareholder (>5%) or affiliate thereof
- No material business relationship with Company/WID Ltd (last 3 years)
- No close family member in executive role
- Not employed by auditor or major advisor

### 2.3 Diversity

Board shall seek diversity in:
- Gender (target ≥30% women by 2026)
- Skills (technology, finance, media, law, governance)
- Geography (Nigeria + international)
- Age and background

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### 3.1 Collective Responsibilities

| Area | Key Duties |
|------|------------|
| **Strategy** | Approve 3-year strategic plan; annual business plan; monitor execution |
| **Financial** | Approve annual budget; quarterly forecasts; capital allocation >₦100M; dividend policy |
| **Risk** | Oversee risk management framework; approve risk appetite; monitor compliance |
| **Governance** | Board composition; succession planning; policies; delegation authority |
| **People** | CEO appointment/removal; C-suite approval; compensation philosophy; culture |
| **Stakeholders** | Creator ecosystem health; user trust; regulatory compliance; ESG |
| **Performance** | KPI dashboard review; CEO evaluation; Board self-assessment |

### 3.2 Reserved Matters (Board Only — Not Delegable)

- Strategy & annual plan approval
- Budget approval (annual + material revisions)
- Capital expenditure >₦100M single item
- Share issuance, buybacks, options
- CEO appointment/removal
- C-suite appointments
- Auditor appointment
- Dividend declaration
- M&A, JV, strategic partnerships >₦100M
- Articles/Memorandum amendments
- Winding up / dissolution
- Related party transactions >₦10M

### 3.3 Chair Responsibilities

- Lead Board; set agenda with CEO
- Ensure effective meetings; manage dynamics
- Facilitate director development
- Liaise with WID Ltd
- Conduct Board/CEO evaluations

### 3.4 Managing Director / CEO Responsibilities

- Day-to-day management per delegation authority
- Execute Board-approved strategy
- Prepare Board papers; ensure information flow
- Build executive team; drive culture
- Represent Company externally
- Report material matters promptly

### 3.5 Non-Executive Director Responsibilities

- Constructive challenge; independent judgment
- Monitor executive performance
- Contribute expertise; mentor executives
- Chair/serve on committees
- Represent shareholder (WID Nominee) / independent perspective

## 4. Board Meetings

### 4.1 Schedule & Frequency

| Meeting Type | Frequency | Notice | Quorum |
|--------------|-----------|--------|--------|
| **Scheduled** | Quarterly (min) | 7 days | 2 directors incl. WID Nominee |
| **Strategy Session** | Annual (2 days) | 14 days | All directors |
| **Ad Hoc** | As needed | 48 hours | 2 directors incl. WID Nominee |

### 4.2 Meeting Procedures

**Agenda:**
- Set by Chair + CEO (circulated 5 days prior)
- Standard items: Minutes, CEO Report, Financials, Risk, Strategy, Committees, AOB

**Papers:**
- Distributed 3 business days before meeting
- Electronic via secure board portal
- Late papers only by Chair consent

**Conduct:**
- Chair facilitates; all directors contribute
- Conflicts declared; interested directors recuse
- Minutes by Company Secretary; circulated 5 days post-meeting
- Actions tracked; status reported next meeting

**Virtual Attendance:**
- Permitted (video preferred)
- Counts toward quorum
- Recording for minutes only (deleted after approval)

### 4.3 Voting

- Majority of directors present and voting
- Chair has casting vote
- Written resolutions valid if all eligible directors sign
- WID Nominee votes per WID Ltd instruction on reserved matters

## 5. Committees

### 5.1 Standing Committees

| Committee | Chair | Composition | Key Duties |
|-----------|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Audit & Risk** | Independent NED | ≥2 NEDs, 1 WID Nominee | Financial reporting; internal controls; audit; risk framework; compliance; whistleblowing |
| **Compensation & Nomination** | Independent NED | ≥2 NEDs | CEO/ exec compensation; Board composition; succession; ESOP; diversity |
| **Technology & Product** | MD/CEO + CTO | MD, CTO, 1 NED | Technical strategy; product roadmap; security; scalability; innovation |
| **Strategy & Investment** | Chair | Chair, MD, WID Nominee, 1 NED | Strategic plan; M&A; major investments; partnerships; capital allocation |

### 5.2 Committee Rules

- Terms of Reference approved by Board
- Minimum 2 members; Chair appointed by Board
- Meet quarterly (min); report to Board
- Minutes circulated to all directors
- No delegated decision-making (recommendations only)

### 5.3 Ad Hoc Committees

Formed as needed (e.g., Fundraising, Litigation, Crisis). Dissolved on completion.

## 8. Conflicts of Interest

### 8.1 Policy

- Directors must avoid actual/perceived conflicts
- Disclose at appointment and annually (Register of Interests)
- Declare at each meeting if relevant
- Recuse from discussion/voting on conflicted matters

### 8.2 Related Party Transactions

- All RPTs >₦10M require Board approval (interested directors excluded)
- Arm's length terms documented
- WID Ltd transactions: Shareholder Agreement governs

### 8.3 Gifts & Hospitality

- Decline gifts >₦50,000 (report to Company Secretary)
- Hospitality: reasonable, business-related, reciprocal

## 10. Risk Management

### 10.1 Board Oversight

- Approve risk appetite statement (annual)
- Monitor top 10 risks quarterly
- Deep-dive on 2–3 risks per meeting
- Ensure risk culture embedded

### 10.2 Key Risk Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Strategic** | Market shifts, competition, technology disruption |
| **Financial** | Liquidity, revenue concentration, FX, fraud |
| **Operational** | Platform downtime, data breach, content liability |
| **Regulatory** | NDPR, NBC, CBN, CAMA, tax |
| **Reputational** | Creator disputes, content moderation, privacy |
| **Talent** | Key person loss, hiring, culture |

### 10.3 Audit & Risk Committee Role

- Monitor risk register
- Review internal audit plan/reports
- Oversee external audit
- Whistleblower channel oversight

---

## 10. Succession Planning

### 10.1 CEO Succession

- **Emergency:** Interim CEO named (WID Nominee + Chair)
- **Planned:** 12–18 month process; internal + external candidates
- **Development:** Identified successors get stretch assignments, mentoring, Board exposure

### 10.2 Board Succession

- Skills matrix reviewed annually
- Pipeline of candidates maintained
- Term limits: 3 × 3-year terms (independent); WID Nominee at pleasure
