# Memorandum of Association

> **Constitutional document defining NovaFlix's objects, powers, and structure**

---

## 1. Name Clause

The name of the Company is **NovaFlix** (hereinafter referred to as "the Company").

---

## 2. Registered Office Clause

The registered office of the Company shall be situated in **Lagos, Nigeria**.

---

## 3. Objects Clause

### 3.1 Principal Objects

**3.1.1 Video Streaming Platform**
To design, develop, deploy, operate, and maintain a global video streaming platform ("NovaFlix") accessible via web, mobile, and connected TV applications, enabling the streaming of films, television series, short-form videos, live streams, and user-generated content.

**3.1.2 Creator Economy & Monetization**
To provide creators with comprehensive monetization tools including but not limited to:
- Subscription-based revenue (recurring monthly/annual)
- Pay-per-minute viewing with automatic wallet crediting
- Tipping, gifting, and virtual goods ("Glow Gifts")
- Membership tiers with exclusive benefits
- Merchandise sales (print-on-demand integration)
- Course and educational content hosting
- Live event ticketing (virtual and hybrid)
- Advertising revenue share (AVOD model)

**3.1.3 Multi-Gateway Payment Infrastructure**
To build and operate a payment orchestration layer integrating:
- **Stripe** — Global cards, wallets, Stripe Connect for creator payouts, multi-currency settlement
- **Paystack** — Nigerian cards, bank transfers, USSD, mobile money, local payouts
- **Flutterwave** — Africa-wide mobile money (M-Pesa, MoMo), cards, bank transfers, barcode payments
- Automated gateway routing by geography, currency, and user preference
- Unified reconciliation, chargeback management, and fraud prevention

**3.1.4 Content Acquisition & Distribution**
To acquire, license, produce, and distribute audio-visual content including:
- Licensed films and television series from studios and distributors
- Independent filmmaker content via revenue-share agreements
- User-generated content (UGC) with creator attribution
- YouTube import and synchronization for creator-owned channels
- Live sports, concerts, and event streaming
- Educational and documentary content

**3.1.5 Technology Licensing & White-Label**
To license the NovaFlix streaming stack (player, CMS, monetization, analytics) to third-party media companies, telcos, and brands on a SaaS or revenue-share basis.

### 3.2 Ancillary Objects

To do all such other things as are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects, including but not limited to:

| Category | Activities |
|----------|------------|
| **Corporate** | Enter partnerships, joint ventures, strategic alliances; acquire or merge with complementary businesses; establish subsidiaries globally |
| **Financial** | Borrow money, issue debentures, grant security, invest surplus funds, provide guarantees for subsidiaries |
| **IP & Technology** | Register trademarks, patents, designs; license IP in/out; develop proprietary algorithms, recommendation engines, DRM |
| **Talent** | Employ, contract, and engage directors, officers, employees, consultants, advisors globally |
| **Marketing** | Advertise, promote, sponsor events; operate referral/affiliate programs; conduct market research |
| **Compliance** | Obtain licenses, permits, approvals; comply with data protection (NDPR, GDPR), broadcasting (NBC), payment (CBN) regulations |
| **Social Impact** | Support creator education, diversity initiatives, cultural preservation; operate creator funds/grants |

## 8. Powers of the Company

1. To enter into contracts, deeds, and agreements
2. To acquire, hold, and dispose of property (real, personal, intellectual)
3. To borrow, lend, guarantee, and provide security
4. To sue and be sued in its corporate name
5. To have a common seal (and use it per Articles)
6. To do all acts necessary for the attainment of its objects
