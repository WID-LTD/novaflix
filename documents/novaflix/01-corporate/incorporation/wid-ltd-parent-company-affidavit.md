## 2. Affirmations

**I, IKE WISDOM, Chief Executive Officer of WID Ltd (RC 8824091), DO SOLEMNLY AND SINCERELY DECLARE AND AFFIRM AS FOLLOWS:**

### 2.1 Corporate Existence

1. **WID Ltd** is a company duly incorporated under the **Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020** of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with Registration Number **RC 8824091**, on the 15th day of October 2024.

2. The registered office of WID Ltd is situate at **Lagos, Nigeria**.

3. WID Ltd carries on business as a strategic African technology and investment corporation with interests in:
   - **Xperiencestore** — E-commerce & Retail Platform
   - **First Lady Fashion Hub** — Fashion Retail
   - **WID Force** — Technology Hub & R&D
   - **Xperience TV** — Media & Streaming
   - **GiGoAI** — AI/ML Solutions (VTON, 3D, Chatbots)
   - **NovaFlix** — Video Streaming Platform (Subsidiary, 100% owned)

### 2.2 Subsidiary Incorporation Authorization

4. At a duly convened meeting of the Board of Directors of WID Ltd held on **15th August 2024**, the Board passed **Resolution WID/2024/001** authorizing the incorporation of a wholly-owned subsidiary under the name **"NovaFlix"**.

5. The resolution authorized:
   - Authorized share capital of **₦10,000,000** (Ten Million Naira)
   - Subscription by WID Ltd for **1,000,000 Ordinary Shares** of **₦1.00** each (₦1,000,000 paid-up)
   - Appointment of **Chukwu Akachukwu Success** as Managing Director of NovaFlix
   - Appointment of **Ike Wisdom** as Non-Executive Director (WID Nominee)
   - Execution of all incorporation documents by Chukwu Akachukwu Success as Authorized Signatory

### 2.3 Shareholding & Control

6. **WID Ltd is the sole legal and beneficial owner** of **1,000,000 Ordinary Shares** in NovaFlix, representing **100% of the issued share capital** at incorporation.

7. WID Ltd exercises **full control** over NovaFlix through:
   - 100% voting rights
   - Board representation (WID Nominee)
   - Protective provisions in NovaFlix Articles of Association
   - Shareholder approval rights for reserved matters

### 2.4 Financial Commitment

8. WID Ltd has paid the sum of **₦1,000,000** (One Million Naira) as subscription consideration for the initial share allotment in NovaFlix.

9. WID Ltd undertakes to provide such further financial support as may be required for NovaFlix's operations, subject to Board approval and compliance with applicable law.

### 2.5 Governance & Oversight

10. WID Ltd shall exercise its shareholder rights in NovaFlix in accordance with:
    - The Memorandum & Articles of Association of NovaFlix
    - The Shareholder Agreement between WID Ltd and NovaFlix
    - Applicable Nigerian law (CAMA 2020, etc.)
    - Good corporate governance practices

11. WID Ltd appoints **Ike Wisdom** as its Nominee Director on the Board of NovaFlix, with the rights and obligations set out in the Articles of Association.

### 2.6 Authorized Signatory

12. **Chukwu Akachukwu Success** is hereby authorized to:
    - Execute all incorporation documents for NovaFlix on behalf of WID Ltd
    - Act as Authorized Signatory for WID Ltd in all matters relating to NovaFlix
    - Sign share certificates, board resolutions, and statutory filings
    - Represent WID Ltd's interests in NovaFlix governance
