## 4. Resolutions Passed

### **RESOLUTION 1: INCORPORATION OF NOVAFLIX**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:**

> WID Ltd incorporates a private company limited by shares under the name **"NovaFlix"** (or such other name as approved by CAC) in the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with the principal objects of:
>
> - Operating a video streaming platform and creator economy business
> - Providing monetization tools for content creators
> - Building multi-gateway payment infrastructure (Stripe, Paystack, Flutterwave)
> - Acquiring, licensing, and distributing audio-visual content
> - Licensing streaming technology to third parties

### **RESOLUTION 2: SHARE CAPITAL & SUBSCRIPTION**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:**

> The authorized share capital of NovaFlix shall be **₦10,000,000** (Ten Million Naira) divided into **10,000,000 Ordinary Shares** of **₦1.00** each.
>
> WID Ltd shall subscribe for **1,000,000 Ordinary Shares** (One Million shares) at par value **₦1.00** each, for a total subscription consideration of **₦1,000,000** (One Million Naira), to be paid in full in cash upon incorporation.

### **RESOLUTION 3: FIRST DIRECTORS**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** the following persons be appointed as the first directors of NovaFlix:

| Name | Role | Nationality | Appointment |
|------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Chukwu Akachukwu Success** | Managing Director / CEO | Nigerian | Executive |
| **Ike Wisdom** | Non-Executive Director (WID Nominee) | Nigerian | Non-Executive |

### **RESOLUTION 4: AUTHORIZED SIGNATORY**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:**

> **Chukwu Akachukwu Success** is hereby appointed as **Authorized Signatory** for WID Ltd for all purposes relating to the incorporation and initial setup of NovaFlix, including but not limited to:
>
> - Execution of CAC incorporation forms (CAC 1.1, etc.)
> - Signing Memorandum & Articles of Association
> - Executing Shareholder Agreement
> - Opening bank accounts
> - Signing statutory declarations and affidavits
> - Appointing legal practitioners for filing
> - Any other documents necessary for incorporation and initial operations

### **RESOLUTION 6: OPERATIONAL AUTHORITIES**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** the Managing Director of NovaFlix (Chukwu Akachukwu Success) is authorized to:

| Authority | Limit | Approval Required |
|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| Operational expenditure | ≤ ₦5,000,000 per transaction | None |
| Operational expenditure | > ₦5,000,000 ≤ ₦25,000,000 | WID Ltd CEO approval |
| Operational expenditure | > ₦25,000,000 | WID Ltd Board approval |
| Contract execution (routine) | ≤ ₦10,000,000 | None |
| Contract execution (strategic) | > ₦10,000,000 | WID Ltd Board approval |
| Hiring (non-executive) | Any | HR process |
| Hiring (executive/C-suite) | Any | WID Ltd Board approval |
| Payment gateway agreements | Any | WID Ltd CEO notification |
| Legal proceedings | Any | WID Ltd Board notification |

### **RESOLUTION 7: PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** the Articles of Association of NovaFlix shall include protective provisions requiring **WID Ltd prior written consent** for the following matters (so long as WID Ltd holds ≥25% of issued share capital):

- Amendment of Memorandum/Articles
- Share issuance / option grants (dilution below 51%)
- Share buyback / redemption
- Dividend policy change
- Appointment/removal of CEO
- Annual budget approval
- Capital expenditure >₦100M
- Borrowing / guarantees >₦50M
- M&A, asset sale, JV >₦100M
- Related party transactions >₦10M
- Change of business
- Winding up / dissolution
- Auditor change
- Registered office change

### **RESOLUTION 8: INFORMATION RIGHTS**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** WID Ltd shall be entitled to:

- Monthly management accounts (by 15th of following month)
- Quarterly board packs (5 business days before meeting)
- Annual audited financial statements
- Immediate notice of material events
- Observer at Board meetings (non-voting)
- Access to books and records upon reasonable notice

### **RESOLUTION 9: FUNDING COMMITMENT**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** WID Ltd commits to:

1. **Initial Subscription:** ₦1,000,000 (paid at incorporation)
2. **Working Capital Facility:** Up to ₦50,000,000 (convertible loan or equity) subject to business plan approval
3. **Follow-on Investment:** Subject to milestone achievement and Board approval

### **RESOLUTION 10: GENERAL AUTHORITY**

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** any one director of WID Ltd or the Company Secretary be authorized to do all acts and things necessary to give effect to the foregoing resolutions, including executing documents, filing with CAC, and making necessary applications.
